What this app is

Period Tracker lets you track menstrual cycles for yourself and your daughter, with two profiles in one app. You can log period days, flow, symptoms, mood, pain, medication and notes, and see predictions for your next period, fertile window and ovulation.

What data the app collects and stores

Cycle and health information you enter: period dates, flow, symptoms, mood, pain level, medication and notes, for each profile. App preferences you set, such as profile names, colours and typical cycle length.

In the Play Store version, this data is stored on your device. It is not sent to the developer, not sold, and not shared with third parties for advertising.

What the app does not do

No account is required. No ads and no third-party advertising trackers in this version. No in-app purchases in this version. The app does not share your period or health data with other apps, advertisers, or data brokers.

Predictions and medical disclaimer

Predictions are estimates based only on the cycles you log. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you have concerns about your cycle, pain, or health, please talk to a clinician.

Children's data

The app is designed for a parent to track cycles for themselves and their daughter, in profiles the parent manages on their own device. The app does not knowingly collect data from children directly, and does not share profile data with anyone.

Deleting your data

You can delete a logged day or clear a profile's history from inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here, with a new last-updated date.
